At the start of your turn ▼
  • Recharge Energy
  • Refresh any exhausted Technologies, Modules or Deployables
  • Draw 5 cards from your Deck. Play 1 Technology (* see Science). Discard the remainder.
  • Move any Modules (up to Speed distance)
  • Destroy any Modules that do not meet their requirements
During your turn ▼
  • Activate Technologies, Deployable or Module effects (pay Energy where required)
    • Unless otherwise noted, a Technology, Deployable or Module cannot use its passive or activate when Exhausted.
  • When you Create Deployables and Modules you place them on the board
    • Modules cannot share the space with other objects. They can be built in any empty space that meets their Requirements.
    • Deployables can share a location with objects. They can be built in any adjacent space to your ship.
    • Unless otherwise noted, "Adjacent" means horizontally/vertically adjacent NOT diagonal.
