RULES

At the start of your turn

  • Refresh any exhausted Technologies, Modules or Deployables.
  • Draw X cards from your Deck (4 + Cyber). Play 1 Technology. Discard the remainder.

During your turn

  • Activate Technologies, Deployable or Module effects.
    • A Tech, Module or Deployable cannot use passive or active when Exhausted or if it does not meet its requirements (all Modules require being somehow connected to your Core).
  • When you Create Deployables and Modules, you place them in an empty location.
    • Modules cannot share the space with other objects. They may not move into other Modules. If they move onto a Deployable's space it is removed.
    • Deployables can share the space with other small objects. They may not move into the space with other large objects.

End of Your Turn

  • Destroy any Modules that do not meet their requirements.

Definitions

  • Unless otherwise noted, "Adjacent" means horizontally/vertically adjacent NOT diagonal.
  • Line of Sight - Centre of your module to the centre of their module. If it crosses ANY part of an occupied square, LoS is blocked.
  • Core Modules - By default ships start with 2 Core Modules. If you lose either Core Module your ship is destroyed.
